PRIVACY POLICY

WHAT WE STORE AND WHY — SHORT VERSION

IF YOU PLAY WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT

Your pilot progression (XP, achievements) stays in your browser's local storage. Leaderboard scores store only the callsign you type. Nothing else leaves your machine.

IF YOU CREATE A REWARDS ACCOUNT

DATAWHY
Email addressAccount login, verification, password reset, payout notifications
PasswordStored only as a salted scrypt hash — never in readable form
IP addressesRate limiting and one-account-per-person checks; stored with your device record and fraud flags
Device fingerprintA hash of browser/device signals (screen, language, timezone, hardware hints) used to detect duplicate accounts. The raw signals are not stored, only the combined hash
Play activityHeartbeat counts, points earned, and run outcomes — the reward ledger
UPI IDPaying out approved rewards to you
18+ declaration timestampLegal requirement for money-adjacent features

WHAT WE DON'T DO

  • No selling or sharing of your personal data with third parties.
  • No tracking of ad impressions or clicks tied to your account.
  • No automated payout decisions — every payout is human-reviewed.
  • No third-party analytics on the game pages.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Sign-up and payout forms use Cloudflare Turnstile for bot protection. If Google login is enabled, Google processes that sign-in under its own policy. Email delivery runs through a transactional email provider (Resend or Brevo).
