HOW TO PLAY

01
MOVE & SHOOT

WASD to move, mouse to aim, hold left-click to fire. On touch: left thumb moves, right thumb aims and fires. Dash with SPACE for invincibility frames.

02
SURVIVE & DRAFT

Every cleared wave offers 3 upgrades. Stack them into a build — multishot, piercing rails, orbiting sawblades. Elites glow gold: kill them for triple score.

03
OVERDRIVE & RANK

Kills fill the gold OVERDRIVE meter — press E for slow-mo double damage. Die, submit your score, earn XP, level up, run it back with R.
